Why does RailSync ML win against Static Schedules?

Static NTES assumes constant delay: if a train is 30m late at Dhanbad, it naively projects it will be 30m late at New Delhi. In reality, Indian Railways timetables include built-in slack before major junctions, and drivers speed up (recovering 30-50% delay). RailSync’s LightGBM regressor models this non-linear recovery curve with sub-4 minute accuracy.
